IMAGE REQUIREMENTS:
-------------------
✓ Format: WebP
✓ Dimensions: 800x600px (2x for retina displays)
✓ Quality: 80-85% compression
✓ Target file size: 20-50KB per image
✓ Aspect ratio: 4:3
✓ Clear, well-lit, professional quality
✓ No watermarks or overlay text

IMAGE SOURCING OPTIONS:
-----------------------
1. Direct from store websites (press/media pages)
2. Contact store marketing teams through your partnership
3. High-quality stock photos (Unsplash, Pexels)
4. Commission professional photography
5. Store's official social media accounts

OPTIMIZATION TOOLS:
-------------------
- TinyPNG.com or Squoosh.app for WebP conversion
- Photopea.com (free online editor)
- Canva.com for resizing
- ImageOptim (Mac) or FileOptimizer (Windows)

AFTER SOURCING:
---------------
1. Optimize each image to WebP format at 800x600px
2. Name exactly as shown above (case-sensitive)
3. Place in /public directory
4. Test page loading and image quality
5. Verify responsive behavior on mobile/tablet/desktop

The page will use fallback images until you add the actual store photos.
