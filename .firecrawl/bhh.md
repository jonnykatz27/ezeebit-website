ARCHITECTS OF TIME

Since our establishment in 1996 as a distributor of high-end watches in South Africa, Architects of Time expanded into luxury retail. In 2007 we opened the doors to our first multi-brand boutique, Boutique Haute Horlogerie, showcasing Chopard, Parmigiani, Zenith, Hermes, Dior, and Ebel timepieces as well as Messika Jewellery.

In 2020 our BHH Boutique flagship Boutique became a reality. Bigger and better than ever, our new concept store has a dedicated section to Chopard as well as a multi-brand gallery with 10 of the most prestigious and exclusive watch and jewelry Brands in the world.  Our latest additions to the BHH portfolio are the legendary Swiss brands Ulysse Nardin and Moser & Cie.
